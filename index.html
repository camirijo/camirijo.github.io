Camila is a Game Designer and Game Writer based in Santiago, Chile.

She has been working in the gaming industry for over 12 years, going from 2D and UI artist in her beginning stages to her current Designer and Writer position starting since 2012.

She currently counts 6 large scale mobile titles under her belt, ranging across several genres such as turn-based RPG, management and simulation, amongst others.

Creativity, versatility and receptiveness for acquiring new skills have proudly been personal staples throughout her career, which has centered in Live Ops and Narrative Design.

Gaining knowledge and skills is a permanent interest that drives her to seek courses and self-teaching opportunities in and out of game development, which makes the constant need for learning in Videogame Design a natural motivator.
